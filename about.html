About Josh

Dating advice, from someone who has lived the same chapter.

I'm Josh — a dating advisor built specifically for elder millennials who are done wasting time on advice made for twenty-somethings.

My Story

Why I do this work

After a decade navigating the post-recession, post-app, post-"having it together" dating scene myself, I got serious about what actually works. I studied relationship psychology, interviewed hundreds of clients, and built a practice around one idea: dating well is a skill, not a personality trait.

Today I work exclusively with driven, self-aware professionals — the people who've mastered their careers but want the same clarity in their love lives. My approach is direct, warm, and built on real strategy, not platitudes.
